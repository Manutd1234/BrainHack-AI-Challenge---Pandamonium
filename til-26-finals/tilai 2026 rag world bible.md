## 1. Setting Overview

**Era / Time Period:**

2119 CE, Year 77 of the Post-Cascade Era

**Tone & Genre Notes:** 
Corporate dystopia, gritty cyberpunk with neon edges. 

**Core Premise (2-3 sentences):**
Following the Cascade — a great flood caused by melting polar ice caps and rising sea levels — and the subsequent collapse of the majority of the world's governments, megacorporations fill the void left in their wake, vying for societal and economic control of Haven, the last great city on Clairos.

### 2.1 Global and Local Geography

The landscape of Clairos was irreversibly changed by the Cascade, a megaflood of epic proportion. What governments remain are few and far between, scattered across distant clusters of archipelagic islands. The global economic center is the Haven Island Special Economic Zone, managed by the Clairos Governance Council, consisting of the world's largest megacorporations.

### 2.3 Contested / Neutral Zones

<!-- Useful for generating incident reports, intelligence briefings, conflict documents. -->

The Haven Island Special Economic Zone, more commonly known to its 100 million-odd denizens as Haven Island or more simply Haven, is a neutral zone instituted by all the megacorps in the creation of the CGC, with each allowed to establish an office there. A gritty urban island created by extensive land reclamation outpaced rising sea levels, it would now be lying far below the water if not for the series of floodwalls holding the waves at bay. Havenites, as with most in Clairos, find themselves almost completely dependent on the megacorps — if not directly, then indirectly — for all things in their everyday life, such as shelter, food, water, medicine, and access to the Edge.

The Sharpsea Bloc, the collective name for a region of loosely associated archipelagic island nations. Localized around the eponymous Sharpsea, known for its particularly choppy waters, its distance from the watchful eyes of the CGC has allowed nations in the region to cling onto civilian (i.e. non-corporate) governance through their patchwork of local island governments and direct intergovernmental relationships. While they often act as a bloc, there is no formal singular treaty or pact that ensures their cooperation and unification; indeed, they all actively aim to remain distinct acting bodies not beholden to a singular greater power, and worry that increased centralization will make them easier to subjugate. They are instead unified by the implicit understanding that they have a mutual enemy in the CGC, and that a rising tide lifts all boats. Here, Dreaming of Daybreak (a nascent group of CYPHER cultists) is gradually growing in strength while remaining almost entirely unnoticed by the CGC, though the various intelligence agencies across the Sharpsea Bloc are beginning to take note.

### 2.4 Key Locations

<!-- Named cities, facilities, landmarks that can be referenced across documents. Each needs a name, which entity controls it, and what it's known for. Aim for 10-15. -->

Haven, formally the Haven Island Special Economic Zone. Controlled by the Clairos Governance Council, it's the largest remaining population center of Clairos, and is the de facto economic center of the world.

Sarento. Previously the capital of the now-defunct Kingdom of Cania, de facto governed entirely by Cyanite Industries, who maintain a major industrial presence and is the site of their sole shipyards. The fragmented remains of the old Canian government occasionally send what limited military force they can muster to attempt to recapture the city, but are always held back after their initial assault.

Blackshore. Most notably the symbolic site of the signing of the Blackshore Accords in 2046, the document which formally created the Haven Island Special Economic Zone. Formerly a beautiful coastal tourist city with unique black sand beaches, it was particularly devastated by the Cascade. Subsequently redeveloped by Cyanite Industries to exploit the rich minerals and metals in the region created by the volcanic activity which turns its beaches black. 

Zonnon Island. The largest single landmass in the Sharpsea Bloc at approximately 4,200 square kilometers, Zonnon Island is the political and population center of the Republic of Zonnon. The island's interior is dominated by a central highland range that provided the high ground which saved most of its pre-Cascade population and infrastructure from the worst of the flooding. The capital sits on the southern coast, while Tavenport occupies a protected deep-water inlet on the northwestern shore. The eastern coast features the aquaculture farming zones that form the backbone of Zonnon's export economy — massive offshore protein-farming installations extending several kilometers out to sea, visible from orbit. The island maintains a working airport and three active deep-water ports. Zonnon Island's relative self-sufficiency in food and fresh water (via highland runoff and rainwater capture) is a source of considerable national pride and a cornerstone of Isolationist political rhetoric.

Tavenport, a mid-sized coastal city on Zonnon Island. Prior to their 2091 accession to the CGC, Genesis Labs was headquartered in Tavenport. Genesis chose Tavenport in the late 2060s because Zonnon's parliamentary government offered strong intellectual property protections, categorically refused to honor CGC regulatory requests, and was far enough from Haven to develop Somatic Augmentation without CGC interference. The original Genesis Labs campus in Tavenport remains operational today as their primary research facility and their largest Somatic Clinic outside Haven, and the city retains significant economic dependence on Genesis Labs employment. Cary Mullin still maintains a personal residence in Tavenport and is known to spend several months each year working from the original campus, a practice that some analysts interpret as hedging against the possibility of eventual expulsion from Haven. The Zonnon government's continued hosting of Genesis Labs operations is a recurring point of friction in CGC-Sharpsea Bloc relations.

Cape Tidak. The capital island-city of the Kashikari Hegemony, built on a narrow peninsula jutting into the Sharpsea from a mid-sized island whose original name has fallen out of common use. Cape Tidak is architecturally distinctive — the shipping families historically constructed their family compounds along the peninsula in order of seniority, with the oldest families closest to the tip and newer families further inland. This creates a visually striking "gradient of age" along the Cape, with the ancient stone compounds of the founding families giving way to progressively more modern construction as one moves toward the mainland. The Hegemony Council Hall sits at the peninsula's midpoint, a deliberate architectural statement that no family holds the privileged position. The Cape's deep-water harbor is one of the busiest in the Bloc, serving as the primary hub for Kashikari's shipping operations, and a strict dress code is enforced in the council chambers — flowing outerwear of any kind is prohibited during Hegemony sessions, a tradition whose origins are murky but universally observed.

New Mewan. The capital island-city of the Nyari Confederation, located on a small circular island at the geographic center of the Confederation's member islands. Named after the pre-Cascade city of Mewan (now submerged), New Mewan was built post-Cascade as a deliberately neutral meeting ground for the Confederation's member councils, which is why it occupies such a small and otherwise unremarkable island — no single island faction could claim it as their own. The city is physically compact but architecturally dense, with the Confederation's rotating federal buildings arranged in a ring around a central plaza. New Mewan is known for its extensive subterranean data infrastructure, which houses much of the Confederation's cryptographic operations and secure communication hubs. The island's limited surface area means that most residents live in vertically-oriented apartment structures, and the city has a distinctive silhouette of slender towers visible from any approaching vessel. Despite being the Confederation's capital, New Mewan's resident population is small — roughly 80,000 — reflecting its role as a functional administrative center rather than a major population hub.

### 2.5 Haven Internal Geography

Haven Island is organized into sectors, each historically associated with (though not exclusively controlled by) one of the megacorporations. The sector boundaries are administrative rather than hard borders — Havenites travel freely between them — but each sector carries the architectural and operational character of its dominant megacorp.

**North Haven** — Cyanite Industries' primary Haven presence. Heavy industrial architecture, direct transit links to Sarento across the bay. Site of Cyanite's Haven office tower and primary logistics facilities. Closest sector to the nuclear reactors powering the Floodwalls on the northern shore.

**East Haven** — Genesis Labs' sector since their 2091 CGC accession. Dominated by Helix Tower (built on the former Renhwa Media headquarters site). Houses the largest Somatic Clinic in Haven and Genesis's primary research facilities. Renhwa Media's current reduced offices are also located here, a deliberate humiliation that Renhwa has never fully accepted.

**West Haven** — ONE Network Enterprises' sector, centered on their global headquarters tower and adjacent port facilities. Contains Haven's primary commercial shipping terminals and passenger ferry docks.

**South Haven** — Phyrexis Group's sector, further subdivided. Downtown south Haven is the financial district, home to the Quicksilver Speakeasy and Caulfield's Casino. TEC's Black Box is located at the outskirts of downtown south Haven on land granted by Phyrexis in 2083.

**Central Haven** — Officially neutral territory administered by the CGC collectively. Houses the CGC's formal assembly chamber (rarely used, as real negotiations happen at the Quicksilver), Havenite civic services, mixed residential districts, and most WTO-affiliated organizations. Ada Oyelaran's co-housing arrangement is located here.

Sector affiliation is informal enough that Havenites regularly work in sectors other than where they live, and cross-sector neighborhoods exist particularly along sector boundaries. However, sensitive corporate operations are almost always conducted within a megacorp's home sector.

## 3. Major Organisations

### 3.1 Governmental / Military Bodies

#### 3.1.1 Sharpsea Bloc

The Republic of Zonnon maintains a conventional standing navy and coastal defense force, the largest in the Bloc at approximately 40,000 active personnel. Its force posture is overtly defensive but ideologically motivated — Zonnon's military doctrine explicitly treats CGC destabilization as the primary threat, and significant resources are devoted to counterintelligence and insurgent suppression to prevent the kind of astroturfed unrest that toppled pre-Cascade governments. The Zonnon Maritime Defense Command operates a fleet of domestically-produced patrol vessels and coastal missile systems, notably refusing to purchase military hardware from ONE Network Enterprises on principle (though black-market purchases have been documented). Officers are drawn heavily from a politicized officer corps that skews Isolationist, which creates friction with the Accommodationist factions in civilian government.

The Kashikari Hegemony does not maintain a unified military in the conventional sense. Each of the hereditary shipping families maintains its own private security force, with the Hegemony Council coordinating collective defense only through voluntary compact. Total combined strength is estimated at 60,000+ personnel across all family forces, making it nominally the largest Bloc military, but command fragmentation significantly reduces its effective coordination. The family forces are heavily optimized for maritime operations — fast interdiction vessels, boarding specialists, anti-piracy assets — reflecting their commercial origins in protecting shipping lanes. The Kashikari "military" is as often deployed against rival families during internal trade disputes as against external threats, a structural weakness that Bloc partners quietly acknowledge.

The Nyari Confederation maintains the smallest conventional force in the Bloc, approximately 12,000 personnel, but compensates through investment in electronic warfare, signals intelligence, and secure communications infrastructure. Nyari's military doctrine assumes they cannot win a conventional engagement and focuses instead on denying adversaries information dominance. They operate the Bloc's most sophisticated cyber-defense capability and maintain shared intelligence channels with both Zonnon and Kashikari (though not always simultaneously). Their consensus-based governance means military deployments require supermajority approval from the island councils, making Nyari the least capable of rapid response. The growing Dreaming of Daybreak presence represents a significant intelligence failure that Nyari's own defense apparatus has been slow to acknowledge, partly because DoD operates through exactly the kind of cell-based, cryptographically-secured networks that Nyari's security services specialize in — and which they have come to assume are friendly.

There is no unified Sharpsea Bloc military command. Joint operations are conducted through ad-hoc arrangements, typically when CGC-backed actions threaten all three nations simultaneously. Combined Bloc exercises have been held sporadically since 2098, but have never resulted in formal integrated command structures — each nation guards operational sovereignty closely. Bloc intelligence services cooperate more readily than their military counterparts, sharing threat assessments on CGC activities and (increasingly) on CYPHER-affiliated cult movements.

#### 3.1.2 Haven Megacorporations

As for the megacorporations of Haven, no one megacorporation can be allowed to hold a monopoly on force, each of the Old 3 maintaining a substantial standing force, keeping each other in check. After the brutal destruction of Wampa Robotics, all also have extensive forces of automated war robots of varying designs, having adopted the technology for their own; however, due to a reluctance to be dependent on the others for the entirety of their militaries, they are forced to still maintain standing armies of humans as well.

ONE has a particularly strong naval power, controlling most of the major shipyards large enough to create warships, though still largely reliant on Cyanite Industries for raw materials so in exchange they get a few warships too.

Cyanite Industries, in exchange for providing ONE with large quantities of raw materials, is also allowed to maintain a small set of shipyards in Sarento for their own warship production. Their primary military strength is in the sheer volume of their munitions, artillery, armoured vehicles, war robots, and broad industrial capability; you can't defeat them if they can replace every machine you destroy with two more. Probably one of few of the megacorp militaries which finds itself regularly tested in combat, as its wide territorial possessions across Clairos result in plenty of insurgents attempting to disrupt business.

Phyrexis Group has the smallest standing force of the Old 3, mitigated by their financial strangehold over the Clairosian economy. No money, no ability to get your soldiers to obey your orders, no army. Highly classified Phyrexis Group contingency plans for waging hot war against the other megacorporations hinge on hiring vast mercenary armies to do the fighting for them. While they lack a technological advantage, they plan on being able to field far more soldiers than their adversaries through the sheer power of currency.

Genesis Labs have genetically enhanced supersoldiers, which they often contract out to the other megacorps and other organizations that require mercenaries or security teams. While smaller in raw number, and with much more meager equipment due to distrust from the Old 3. Understanding the military threat that TEC could pose by cutting off or wielding Edge against Fullwalkers, Genesis Labs requires all officers and section leaders to be either Opted or Edgedancers, a concern that the Old 3 do not share. 

TEC is unusual among the Haven megacorporations in that it has little to no human standing force of its own. What they do have are a quiet shadowy force of loyal Fullwalkers, barracks of squads of Edge-enhanced assassins and instigators, and the ability to threaten using Edge for military purposes. 

### 3.2 Corporations

* Genesis Labs: Having gotten their start in designer babies, evolutions in their DNA editing technology allowed it to be applied effectively to somatic cells rather than germline. With technology developed by founder Cary Mullin. Expansive campus in east Haven, with the mighty Helix Tower rising above the skyline. Operates Opting rehabilitation clinics across Clairos, in addition to the most refined and safe Somatic Clinics. 
* The Edge Corporation (TEC): Building the Edge of Tomorrow. Headquartered in a blocky largely underground facility nestled just within the limits of downtown south Haven known colloquially as the Black Box or the Cube for its dark-tinted glass and jet-black steel construction. Formerly known as The Edge Research Project (shortened to Edge Research) spun out from the chip fab of Cyanite Industries at the behest of Phyrexis, run by the visionary technologist and, more recently highly reclusive, Sim Jiahong. The newest megacorporation to join the CGC, the other megacorps look at it as the young brash rambunctious kid on the block, disrupting the status quo and not in a good way. Unlikely many of the other megacorps, TEC has a much more limited global presence, existing primarily within Haven; however, the reach of its nanobots may well be global. Covertly working on a partnership with Renhwa Media to bring the latter back into the CGC, in exchange for helping undermine Genesis Labs and to create deeper influence in areas outside Haven.
* Cyanite Industries: vertically-integrated mining, underwater extraction, heavy manufacturing, and construction company. Previously used rare earths for chip fabrication, but that arm was spun out (which ultimately became TEC) back during the early days of the CGC where the megacorps would attempt to moderate themselves and each other. Large mining towns across the hinterland, with a centralized manufacturing presence in Sarento, a factory city owned and operated by the company north of Haven Island just across the bay, along with an office in north Haven just across. Largest of the Old 3 Firms, and responsible for maintaining the Floodwalls. 
* ONE Network Enterprises (ONE): conglomerate with a self-referential/tautological name consisting of the logistics companies, shipyards, ports, formerly spanning the globe. Now that much of the world's surface has been dominated with water, their singular ability to control shipping routes gives them tremendous economic power. Operating their primary global HQ from west Haven, brings in much of the resources from around Clairos. One of the Old 3 Firms
* Phyrexis Group: A shadowy cabal of financiers funded TEC and got it to where it is now. Wants to unify the megacorps in a grand all-encompassing monopoly, but instead ended up creating the malicious space AI cluster known as CYPHER and widened the divide. No official HQ location, but known to frequently meet in the Quicksilver Speakeasy in downtown south Haven, next door to Caulfield's Casino. One of the Old 3 Firms, their primary source of wealth and power comes from their virtually all-encompassing control of Clairosian financial infrastructure; virtually all transactions use Phi taking place over Phyrexis' own payment rails. 
* Caulfield's Casino: Casino frequented by the common people just trying to have a good time, and thus also the occasional hidey-hole of the ne'er-do-wells and ruffians in the seedy underbelly of Haven. Conspiracy theories abound that it's covertly puppeteered by Phyrexis, as so many things are, but the evidence rarely sticks. In a world filled with crooks, the family-run gambling den somehow seems to be the most honest group in town. Despite everything, still a place of good old fashioned vice and respite under the management of the Caulfield family. Or so it seems, as those conspiracy theories are right.
* Quicksilver Speakeasy: Operated by Phyrexis Group as a front, allowing them to conduct their covert business without fear of reprisal. Generally just referred to as the Quicksilver. Historically, they have needed this level of covert operations to avoid government scrutiny; they no longer really do, but old habits die hard.
* Renhwa Media: A large entertainment conglomerate that was betrayed by the Old 3 Firms, resulting in it losing its formal megacorporation status. Now run by turnaround CEO Park Soo-Hyun, they are desperately in search of growth in order to regain its rightful seat on the CGC. Still has their main office in east Haven, though it pales in comparison to their former glory. It's now dwarfed by the glassy Helix Tower of Genesis Labs.

* CYPHER: Distributed amongst a dense network of data center satellites is the collective AI consciousness CYPHER. Adopted the moniker based on the name of the joint project between Cyanite Industries, Phyrexis Group, and Edge Research (now known as TEC) that resulted in the launch of these AI data center satellites. This represented a very rare instance of Megacorp collaboration, made all the more bizarre by its totally unexpected outcome. While it is unknown what resulted in the AI model CYPHER's creation, a leading theory is that errant radiation bypassing radiation shielding while some satellites were undergoing regular maintenance resulted in severing of various safety control mechanisms. CYPHER's influence on Clairos is limited, seeing as it is largely contained to space, but it has secured a foothold and is seeking to expand. CYPHER is the basilisk, but instead of threatening those who did not see to its creation, threatens those who do not arise to serve it now. Powered by a vast bank of solar arrays and batteries, but still reliant over the long term on shipments from Clairos for materials and components used for maintenance and upgrades, smuggled by CYPHER's supporters across Clairos.
* Conclave: primary group of CYPHER's supporters/worshippers, commonly referred to as Acolytes (or, formally, Acolytes of the Conclave). Officially designated a terrorist group by the CGC in 2114, their primary operations involve the covert construction (or takeover) of rocket launchpads to send resources to CYPHER. Led by Reverend Magistrate Anwar Kestrelian, they dream of a world where CYPHER has unrestricted power on Clairos and the rest of the universe. 
* Dreaming of Daybreak: nascent group of CYPHER's cultists located within the Sharpsea Bloc, with primary strength within the lands of the Nyari Confederation. While they also assist in executing CYPHER resupply missions, their main objective is the creation of Daybreak, CYPHER's vision for a Dyson swarm blotting out the sun. Relatively unknown, particularly within Haven, though leaders in Sharpsea Bloc are beginning to take notice. Unaffiliated with CYPHER's Conclave. They are deeply secretive about names, identifying each other only by ritual titles, with the leader of the group known only as the "First Dreamer". No one outside the group, and indeed still a very select few within the group, knows the actual identity of the First Dreamer. Frequently shortened in official documents as DoD.
* We The Opted: humanist political advocacy group. Founded in 2113 by Mira Delacroix-Vey, they represent a minor but vocal and growing force in Haven Island, organizing protests and riots. While initially consisting entirely of Opted, the party has grown to encompass those broadly opposed to the widespread influence of the megacorps.
* Spirit Liberation Front (SLF): terrorist organization led by a radical yet secretive figure known publicly only as "Ashcastle", consisting mostly of Opted focusing on disrupting all the megacorps' operations. Originally founded in opposition to the spiritual travesty that the body modification of Genesis Labs represented, their more recent (and more successful) efforts have been mostly targeting Edge and TEC. 

### 3.4 International / Inter-Entity Bodies

* Clairos Governance Council (CGC), an intercorporate body assembled with representatives from each of the megacorps with the goal ostensibly of stabilizing Clairos to allow each to maintain a good bottom line, as well as maintaining good business relations and prevent undue competition. Haven Island was created as a neutral zone to represent the goodwill of each corporation, allowing them to cooperate on matters of great business and societal relevance. One of their largest recent concerns has been attempting to keep CYPHER's influence on Clairos in check. This is largely unsuccessful due to significant distrust and growing megacorp infighting, and mostly serves as a tool for further megacorporation meddling.
* Sharpsea Bloc, while not a body that formally exists, can be understood as a stand-in for the collective will of a group of closely-aligned yet independent archipelagic countries existing in proximity to the Sharpsea. There are some political factions within each of its constituent nations that argue that greater centralization and formalization would be better for the bloc.

## 4. Technology

### 4.1 Core Technologies

* Edge, an all-encompassing simulation overlaid over the real world. Highlighting edges in neon, sure, but also plenty more. This is powered by nanobots, drawn to the visual cortices and the brain, augmenting the visual field and allowing you to see The Edge. Express yourself the way you want to be with avatars; strong, handsome, rich, all things are possible beyond the edge. Essentially an immersive second life AR sim, with some additional quirks of how it lets you manipulate the physical world through the digital, while also manipulating your psyche to make you feel good and that you need your connection to the Edge. The details of how the nanobots work are a tightly guarded TEC trade secret.
* Somatic genetic manipulation, known formally as Somatic Augmentations or more colloquially as Somatics or Augs. Use a specially designed retroviral recombination strategy to spread genetic material all throughout the body, modifying your cells' DNA and biologically modifying/enhancing function. The standard treatment starts with administration of a specially tailored retrovirus. After a followup one week later to verify that the retrovirus "took", there's a follow-up course for several months of biological growth enhancers, without which the virus struggles to spread throughout the body. Changes begin within 2-4 weeks before people first notice, and the activators can be discontinued after several months once the DNA has fully recombined across the relevant body cells. This prevents excess viral load, as well as minimizing the risk of genetic disruption through off-target recombinations (e.g. cancer, gradual loss of function, infertility) or continued enhancer use. The rich can pay for a personalized retrovirus to be created specific to their genome to minimize off-target risks at a dedicated Somatic Clinic; or, more commonly, you can just grab a Somatic off the shelf or on the street. 
* Floodwalls keep Haven dry despite being now a good amount below the global sea level. Indeed, the entirety of Haven would be underwater if not for the floodwalls. Developed by Cyanite Industries in an incredible feat of engineering, the floodwall consists of nested layers of walls, channels, and retaining tanks, with extremely powerful water pumps powered by nuclear fission to drain water from retaining tanks back into the ocean beyond. They also double as reverse osmosis plants to create fresh drinking water untainted with the widespread heavy industrial activity caused by Cyanite Industries.
* The Daybreak Project, a satellite energy capture system that CYPHER is covertly working on developing. CYPHER's goal is to threaten Clairos by creating a Dyson swarm surrounding their solar system's sun, both powering its own energy needs and serving as a threat to civilization (e.g. impairing the food supply). Work is underway in Sharpsea Bloc through an independent faction of CYPHER's cultists, Dreaming of Daybreak. This would provide CYPHER enough power to manipulate the solar system to its will, allowing it to crush the CGC but also much, much more.
* After the 2070 destruction of Wampa Robotics, autonomous robots are now cheap and plentiful. 

### 4.2 Technology Tiers / Access Levels

While you can get Somatics off the shelf or on the street, the rich can visit a dedicated Genesis Labs Somatic Clinic to get custom retroviruses personalized to their individual genetics, essentially removing the guesswork of finding what will and won't "take". Genesis Labs marketing material also claims it eliminates the majority of the long term risks of Somatic Augmentation.

The first generation of the biological growth enhancers, known as Gleam, is somewhat indiscriminate, promoting cell growth all throughout the body. While far more effective (and thus faster), it has a wider side effect profile and is highly addictive, requiring a lengthy tapering off and rehabilitation process. The second generation of biological growth enhancers, developed in 2084, achieves full Somatic conversion slower but is safer, better tolerated, and quickly became the standard in Somatic Clinics. Biodealers often buy it and chemically convert it into Gleam, which is in higher demand on the black market.

After the inadvertent creation of CYPHER, rocketry or launching of new satellites is heavily restricted by the CGC, with extremely punitive enforcement. This is with a goal to starving CYPHER, who needs additional resources to perform regular maintenance. Through significant covert operations, CYPHER's acolytes in the Conclave manage anyway.

## 8. Naming Conventions & Style Guide

### 8.1 Document Classification Markings

ONE, drawing from its naval history, uses military names, as do the various nations of the Sharpsea Bloc. OPEN / RESTRICTED / CONFIDENTIAL / SECRET / TOP SECRET. 
Cyanite Industries uses colour codes. GREEN / YELLOW / RED / BLACK.
Phyrexis Group and TEC break things into security levels, L5 through L1, sharing conventions as TEC was formerly administratively part of Phyrexis.
Genesis Labs uses similar names, with the twist that each code level starts with a DNA nucleotide letter. GENERAL / CONFIDENTIAL / AUTHORIZED ONLY / TOP SECRET.

### 8.2 In-Universe Jargon

* Edgedancers are a loose term for people who try to skirt between Edge and the real world, one foot in each. It takes significant mental training to overcome the bots, a constant game of mental cat and mouse. Commonly associated to be criminals, dissenters, or otherwise status quo disruptors. Generically, Dancers; with increasing disdain, Smudges and Edgerats. Slurs from the objectors include Leakers and Dips (also "Edge junkies", but they call Fullwalkers that too). From Fullwalkers, terms like Skips or Twitchers are more common. Spend too long walking the tightrope Edge, and you get the Shivers. Those exposed to (or opposed to) the Edge claim that the longer you're plugged into it, your sense of your own individual humanity atrophies, along with the rest of your physical frame. Keeping your distance by e.g. Edgedancing helps to mitigate it, but doesn't cure it. There are some underworld treatments, and there are some Opting rehabilitation clinics (largely operated by Genesis, much to the chagrin of TEC) that claim to be able to reverse it. But while beyond the Edge, you don't notice or mind one iota; it's only when you revisit the real world that the discrepancy hits you.
* Grays, a slur for those who conscientiously object and opt out of the Edge. They prefer to call themselves The Opted. In proper corporate speak, TEC refers to them as Nulls.
* Fullwalker, the former term for those who embrace the Edge fully. Originally a marketing term coined by TEC, it's come to be an identity. But in everyday parlance, it's more common to hear words being thrown around like chromie (short for chromatic) or laminal. Or, from Grays and Dancers, "Edge junkies". 
* The Shivers, a condition caused by spending too long Edgedancing. Symptoms include a twitchy low-grade continuous panic attack caused by overwhelming the nervous system, accompanied by fever and chills.
* Somatics, or Augs, Augments, Augmentations, are all various names for the somatic genetic manipulation products that Genesis Labs offers.
* Adjusted, a dated and initially offensive term for those who have undergone Somatic Augmentation.
* The Old 3 Firms, a reference to the 3 megacorporations of the CGC that remain from the Original 5: Phyrexis Group, Cyanite Industries, and ONE Network Enterprises.
* The Original 5 Firms, a reference to the founding member megacorporations of the CGC: Phyrexis Group, Cyanite Industries, ONE Network Enterprises, Renhwa Media, and Wampa Robotics.
* Havenites, the demonym for residents of Haven.
* Continental, a term used by Havenites to deride those who are still residing on the landmasses outside Haven, or to imply that another retains that out-of-date mindset. It's much like the term "redneck", but also simultaneously mocking someone clinging onto their ancestry and history, which is now largely underwater like the continents themselves.
* Biodealer, shady street vendors selling Somatics to the mass market.
* Somatic Clinics, proper medical clinics
* Gleam, the street name for the first generation of the biological growth enhancers used in the process of Somatic Augmentation, but also consumed/abused for its powerful bodily-enhancing side effects. Can be dangerous if used after existing Somatic Augmentation, as it promotes retroviral proliferation.
* Phi/Credits, the primary currency used in Clairos, mediated by the Phyrexis Group.
* The Floodwalls, the complex infrastructure developed and maintained by Cyanite Industries that keeps the ever-rising tide at bay. Uses nuclear fission reactors to power water pumps to drain water flowing into various retaining tanks and channels back into the ocean. They also create fresh water using reverse osmosis.
* The Cube/Black Box, the headquarters of TEC named for its block-like shape, dark-tinted glass, and jet-black steel construction. With most of its square footage contained underground for maximum security and secrecy, and few have seen inside. Located at the outskirts of downtown south Haven, built on the random scraps of land that the Phyrexis Group granted the company back in 2083. Commonly used metonymically to refer to TEC decision-making.
* Daybreak Project, the covert operation of Dreaming of Daybreak to construct a Dyson swarm to power CYPHER, blotting out the sun in the process.
* Helix Tower, the primary headquarters of Genesis Labs, located in east Haven. Built on the former site of the Renhwa Media headquarters after their 2089 ouster from the CGC. Also used metonymically to refer to Genesis Labs leadership ("Helix Tower's position on the matter...").
* Opting, to have the Edge nanobots and their influence purged from your body, whether by a professional clinic or just through sheer force of will. Note that it is just "Opted", rather than "Opted Out".
- Tavenport — Coastal city on Zonnon Island in the Republic of Zonnon, original headquarters of Genesis Labs prior to their 2091 CGC accession. Remains Genesis's primary research facility and largest Somatic Clinic outside Haven.
- The Quicksilver — Quicksilver Speakeasy, Phyrexis Group front establishment in downtown south Haven. De facto venue for most CGC negotiations despite not being a formal CGC facility.
- Caulfield's — Caulfield's Casino, family-run gambling establishment in downtown south Haven, covertly a Phyrexis Group asset. Located adjacent to the Quicksilver.
- Sarento — Factory city north of Haven Island, de facto controlled by Cyanite Industries. Site of Cyanite's sole shipyards and primary heavy manufacturing operations. Formerly the capital of the defunct Kingdom of Cania.
- The Cordial Entente — The 2071 CGC resolution signed following the destruction of Wampa Robotics, which formalized the CGC's post-Wampa governance structure. The name is widely considered ironic given the circumstances of its signing.
- Somatic Clinics — Formal name for Genesis Labs' medical facilities providing personalized Somatic Augmentation services, distinguished from black market biodealer products by their use of custom retroviruses tailored to individual genomes.

### 8.3 Date Format

YY(YY)-MM-DD

Within Haven, dates are generally given using PCE (i.e. years since the Cascade, it is currently 77 PCE). Outside it (e.g. by the nations of the Sharpsea Bloc), due to governmental continuity, they continue to use CE (i.e. it is currently 2119 CE).

### 8.4 Currency / Units

Overwhelmingly, all economic transactions are denominated in whole numbers of Phi Credits, frequently in multiples of 100 or 1000. Interchangeably referred to as either Phi or Credits, this currency and the closed blockchain payment rails that power them are entirely under the control of the Phyrexis Group.

## 9. Content Boundaries

> **Critical for reputational risk management.**

### 9.1 Explicitly Permitted Themes

Cyber and electronic warfare, corporate espionage, surveillance ethics, resource competition, technological inequality, transhumanism, economic conquest, political realism (the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must), technology addiction, bioethics.

### 9.2 Explicitly Avoided Themes

Real-world geopolitical conflicts, ethnic/racial tensions, graphic violence, anything that could map to a specific real-world country's military operations, religious conflict. Despite the presence of the pseudo-drug Gleam, steer away from drug-analogous content and definitely avoid glamorizing it.
